A QR code doesn't tell you where it leads. SecureQR checks first.

When you scan a code that contains a web link, SecureQR does not open it. Our server visits the link for you, follows every redirect, and takes a picture of the page it ends on. You see the real destination, the picture and any warning signs, and then you decide.

What it looks for
• Where the link really ends, with every stop along the way
• Sites on the threat lists of Quad9, Cloudflare and DNS4EU, and on an open list of active phishing sites
• Pages that ask for a password or card details, are not encrypted, use look-alike characters, or try to download a file
• Links that try to open another app

Payment and other codes
Bank transfers, payment requests, Wi-Fi networks, contacts and sign-in codes are read on your phone and shown in plain words before anything happens. They are never sent to us.

Private by design
• No account, no advertising, no analytics
• Only web links are sent to our server; everything else stays on your phone
• Results and page pictures are kept for an hour and then deleted
• Your scan history stays on your phone, and you can clear it at any time

A clean result means we found no warning signs. It is not a guarantee.
