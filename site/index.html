      These examples are a recording, not a live check, and the sites in them do not exist. The app does this for real with every link you scan.

      How it works

      1

      Scan

      Point the app at a QR code. It reads the code and stops there. Nothing is opened.

      2

      We go first

      Our server opens the link in a locked-down browser, follows every redirect and takes a picture of the page it ends on.

      3

      You decide

      You see the real destination, the picture and any warning signs. Open it, or don't.

      What it looks for

      • Where it really ends. Short links and trackers hide the destination. You see every stop along the way and the final address, with the part that matters highlighted.
      • Known bad sites. Every address on the path is checked against the threat lists of Quad9, Cloudflare and DNS4EU, and against an open list of active phishing sites.
      • Pages that ask for too much. A warning when the page wants a password or card details, is not encrypted, uses look-alike characters, or tries to download a file.
      • Hand-overs to other apps. You are told when a link tries to open another app instead of a web page.
      • Payment and other codes. Bank transfers, payment requests, Wi-Fi, contacts and sign-in codes are read on your phone and shown in plain words before anything happens. They are never sent to us.

      A clean result means we found no warning signs. It is not a guarantee: a site can show our server something different from what it shows you.

      Privacy

      • Only web links reach us. Everything else in a QR code stays on your phone.
      • No account, no tracking. The app works without signing in and has no advertising or analytics.
      • We keep very little. Page pictures are held in memory for 15 minutes and then gone. Our logs record your IP address, as any web server does, plus the site names involved and the result, but not the full link.
      • Your scan history stays on your phone. You can clear it at any time.
      • Blocklist checks send the site names (not the full link) to Quad9, Cloudflare and DNS4EU as ordinary DNS lookups.
      • The full details are in the privacy policy.
      • One caution: checking a link means visiting it. A link that works only once, such as a one-time sign-in link, can be used up by the check. Results are reused for ten minutes, so the same link is not visited again and again.
