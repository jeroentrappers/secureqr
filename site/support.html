Support

Something not working, or a question about a result? Write to privacy@appmire.be. Please include your phone model and what you scanned, if you can share it.

Common questions

The app says "No warning signs found". Is the link safe?

It means we found no warning signs: the site is not on the blocklists we check, and the page did not ask for a password or card details. It is not a guarantee. A site can show our server something different from what it shows you, and a new scam may not be on any list yet. Look at the address and the picture, and only continue if it is what you expected.

Why did a link stop working after I checked it?

Checking a link means visiting it. A link that works only once, such as a one-time sign-in link, can be used up by the check. Ask the sender for a new one and open it directly if you trust where it came from.

"The site did not respond" or "Could not be checked"

The site may be down, may block visits from servers, or may only exist on a private network (for example a router or an office system). You can still open the link yourself if you trust it.

"This copy of the app can no longer use the service"

Your version of the app is too old for our server. Update SecureQR from the App Store or Google Play and try again.

The picture of the page looks different from what I see

The picture is taken by our server, in a phone-sized browser, without your cookies or sign-ins. Sites often show a cookie notice, another language or a sign-in page to a first-time visitor.

The camera does not start

The app needs permission to use the camera. Turn it on in your phone's settings under SecureQR, or use the picture button on the scan screen to read a QR code from a photo instead.

How do I delete my scan history?

Open History from the scan screen and choose "Clear all", or remove single items. The history exists only on your phone.

Does the app pay or sign in for me?

No. For payment codes and sign-in codes the app only shows what the code contains. Nothing is paid, opened or joined without a tap from you.
